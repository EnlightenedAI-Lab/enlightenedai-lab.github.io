Screening Context

RAISE comes to Centre Stage.

The “Raise” film screening is listed in the AI Film Festival programme at AI for Good Global Summit 2026. The screening is scheduled for 9 July 2026, from 18:30 to 20:00 CEST, on Centre Stage in Geneva.

The human question beneath the film.

RAISE places artificial intelligence inside the human question: not simply what AI can do, but what wisdom should guide it. Intelligence without reflection can drift. Capability without judgment can amplify risk. The future of AI depends on the wisdom of humanity teaching, guiding, and governing the intelligence it creates.

Screening details.

Interview Contribution

Enlightened AI Research Lab in the RAISE conversation.

Nicolas Holm was interviewed for RAISE on AI, humanity, ethics, and responsible intelligence. His contribution reflects the lab’s central position: AI should not only become more capable; it should remain connected to evidence, reflection, human judgment, and responsibility.

Interviewed for RAISE
Screening at AI for Good

The deeper question is not whether artificial intelligence can exceed human capability. It is whether human wisdom can teach, guide, and govern the intelligence it creates.

Themes

The ideas carried into the conversation.

These themes connect the RAISE conversation to Enlightened AI Research Lab’s broader work on alignment, reflective stability, moral coherence, and accountable AI reliance.

01

Wisdom before intelligence

Artificial intelligence should be guided by human wisdom, not merely by capability, speed, or scale.

02

Human judgment

AI must remain accountable to human interpretation, evidence, and responsibility.

03

Ethics under scale

As AI becomes more capable, ethical stability becomes a structural problem, not only a policy problem.

04

Responsible intelligence

The future of AI depends not only on performance, but on coherence, reflection, and trust.
