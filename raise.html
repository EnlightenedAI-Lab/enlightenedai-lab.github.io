About RAISE

A global story about AI in the real world.

RAISE is a six-part global documentary series exploring how artificial intelligence is already being used to address urgent human challenges and help build a future worth believing in.

The question beneath the film.

The RAISE project asks whether wisdom must guide the intelligence of the future. That question connects directly to Enlightened AI Research Lab’s central position: AI should not only become more capable; it should remain connected to evidence, reflection, human judgment, and responsibility.

Lab Contribution

A conversation on responsible intelligence.

Nicolas Holm, founder of Enlightened AI Research Lab, was interviewed for the RAISE project on AI, humanity, ethics, and the future of responsible intelligence. The contribution reflects the lab’s broader work on alignment, internal stability, reflective dynamics, and accountable AI reliance.

01

Human judgment

AI must remain accountable to human interpretation, evidence, and responsibility.

02

Ethics under scale

As AI becomes more capable, ethical stability becomes a structural problem, not only a policy problem.

03

Responsible intelligence

The future of AI depends not only on performance, but on coherence, reflection, and trust.

04

Service to humanity

The central question is not whether AI can do more, but whether it can help humanity choose better.
